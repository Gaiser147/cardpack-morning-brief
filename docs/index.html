How it works

One page you publish once, plus one scheduled task that fills it every morning. The page never changes; the task only writes data.

Claude reads your day

A recurring task runs before you wake up. It reads your connected apps, read only: calendar, mail, learning platform, to-dos, work.

It deals 4–9 cards

The one must-do, today's schedule, who's waiting for your reply, what's due this week, a study topic, and the next exam as the boss card. Written as packs/<date> into the artifact's database.

You tear it open

Open the same link every morning. Swipe the strip off, pull out the cards, then swipe through your hand, stamp cards done, follow their links.

Three print series, rotating daily

The same card anatomy in three print styles. Every card also gets a foil, a rim and a wear grade from Mint to Battle-Scarred, rolled from the date, so it looks the same every time you open it.

Your data stays yours

The brief is built to look, not touch.

Read onlyThe task never sends, drafts, submits or changes anything in your calendar, mail or other apps.
Private artifactPacks live in the database of your own artifact. Nobody sees them unless you share the link.
Safe links onlyCard buttons only link to submissions, mail threads or polls. Never money, contracts, health or credentials.
